# ❤️ Supporting PyChem-Pro

Thank you for visiting the PyChem-Pro sponsorship page! 

PyChem-Pro is an open-source, high-performance cheminformatics library, molecular viewer, and molecular descriptor calculation toolkit designed to accelerate academic and industrial research in drug discovery, QSAR modeling, and molecular simulations. 

Maintaining, optimizing, and scaling a library capable of calculating over 500 unique molecular descriptors requires continuous infrastructure testing and development. Financial contributions directly fuel the longevity, reliability, and speed of this software.

---

## 🚀 How Your Support is Reinvested

Every contribution received via our community funding channels is directly funneled back into the project's ecosystem to benefit all users:

*   **Cloud GPU Testing Environments:** Offsetting the costs of running massive batch calculation benchmarks on cloud instances (e.g., AWS, Google Cloud) to guarantee code stability.
*   **Continuous Integration & Compatibility:** Funding automated testing suites to maintain seamless compatibility across upcoming Python releases (Python 3.14+).
*   **Documentation Infrastructure:** Expanding our technical guides from the baseline `Manual.pdf` into a fully interactive, searchable web documentation platform.
*   **Community Feature Bounties:** Providing micro-grants and feature-bounties to student developers and contributors to accelerate requested features.
